About me

  • Full-time City of Clemson resident since 2023; part-time resident since 2017.
  • My parents, Nancy and Chip, and siblings, Catherine and David, moved to Clemson in 1982 and lived here for more than 10 years. My in-laws lived in Clemson for 20+ years.
  • 1986 Clemson graduate with a B.S. in Financial Management.
  • My daughters are fourth-generation Clemson graduates. I am a proud grandfather of three.
  • Married for 40 great years to Kelley Page, my high school sweetheart in Atlanta.
  • Career public finance banker working from home in Clemson.

A history of service

  • Alumni Loyalty Board Chairman (2014–2018) Advisory board to the Clemson University Business School. I remain active as a student mentor.
  • Alumni Advisor (2025–present) Pi Kappa Alpha Fraternity.
  • F3 Clemson “Fitness, Fellowship, and Faith.” Active in a nationwide peer-led workout group that supports community leadership.

Why I’m running

A fresh, thoughtful approach

I have a strong desire to promote accountability and enhanced collaboration with the University, local businesses, and City leaders to drive smart growth in the City.

Real solutions for student housing

I want to lead the Council in addressing the student housing crunch. My background and experience in providing financing to municipalities and universities will produce real and creative solutions.

Safer streets and traffic relief

Introduce badly needed strategies to enhance City infrastructure, improve safety, and provide traffic relief for pedestrians and motorists.

Fiscal responsibility

Ensure fiscal responsibility in government to maximize your tax dollars.

Why I’m qualified

This opportunity is important to me! I want to serve the City where my family has such deep history and where I will reside forever.

My professional background in financing governments and universities will bring a unique and complementary voice to the Council.

As a former Clemson student and current City resident, I will bring a balanced perspective to the issues that impact the City.

My pledge to fellow Clemson residents

I am committed to improving transparency and communication so that residents truly feel the City Council is their Council and represents their voice.
