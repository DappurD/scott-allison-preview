Texting terms

The campaign isn’t sending texts right now. If it starts, these are the terms, and you’ll only get texts if you ask for them.

On this page

  2. Frequency and cost

Optional signup

You only get texts if you ask for them. If the campaign adds a signup form, texts will be a separate box on it that starts unchecked; giving us your number by itself doesn’t sign you up. Every message will say who it’s from.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Texts are optional; you can skip them and still get everything else from the campaign.

Stop or get help

Reply STOP to any text to stop them, or HELP for help.

Privacy

How we handle your number is in the privacy notice.
