In this preview

The form validates your entries on your device and shows a demonstration confirmation. It does not transmit or save the name, email address, phone number, or permissions you enter. This preview has no campaign analytics or advertising pixels. The hosting service may process standard service and security information.

Before a live campaign launch

The campaign must identify the organization responsible for this site, connect an approved signup service, name its service providers, and publish a complete privacy policy.

What the planned signup collects

Email address; optional name and phone number; the type of request; separately selected email and text permissions; the disclosure version and receipt time; and general link-source tags. A phone number alone does not mean consent to recurring texts.

How a live campaign should use it

Respond to the request and provide updates only through the channels the person chooses. Restrict access to campaign staff and approved providers. Publish a contact for access, deletion, and unsubscribe requests before collection begins.

Questions
