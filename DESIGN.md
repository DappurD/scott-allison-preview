# Aurora Bento (final synthesis)

The final site, built from the three judged variants: Bento Editorial's tile system and section layouts, Aurora Civic's dark cover, adaptive glass header and cursor glow, Bold Blocks' section-head device and pill call to action. Same content, ids, ARIA, form contract and copy as the judged builds; `config.js` is unchanged.

## The idea in five lines

1. The page is a stack of three surfaces: a near-black indigo ground with slow aurora light under the cover, a warm cream canvas sheet that rises out of it with large radii (Meet Scott, the deep-indigo Priorities panel, the Our City sunset band, Voting), and the dark ground again for Get Involved and the footer.
2. Everything sits in tiles with hairline gradient borders (violet to coral on white, glass hairlines on dark): the cover's dark-glass instrument bar of four facts, the stat tiles, the four dark-glass priority pillars, the voting bento, the white form tile with its peach-orange-violet ring.
3. Type does the work, in three tiers: (A) Barlow Condensed caps only for the kinetic cream nameplate "SCOTT ALLISON." with its orange stop, the section heads (a violet or peach accent on the key word, no stop), the motto, page titles, the wordmark and the numerals; (B) Manrope 700 in sentence case with tight tracking for every card, form and document heading; (C) Manrope at 18px for everything you read and the 14px tracked eyebrows.
4. One glass pill header adapts to the surface under it: dark frosted over the cover, the priorities panel, Get Involved and the footer; white frosted with a violet-to-coral hairline over the cream canvas. A sliding marker follows the current section; it is the only scroll-position cue in the pill.
5. One object is allowed to be the brightest thing on the page: the orange Election Day card in Voting, joined to Early Voting by a regalia-to-orange timeline rail. Priorities are four identical pillars in a 2x2 matrix (from 375px) with no numbers, badges, tints or order of any kind.

## Motion rules

- Only `transform`, `opacity`, `translate`, `scale` and `rotate` animate (plus colors on hover). `will-change: transform` only on the cover's two aurora layers.
- Load: the nameplate words rise out of clipped lines once (0.9s), then the office, date line, lede, actions, portrait and the fact tiles follow (staggered to 0.7s; from 1000px the instrument bar rises as one piece at 0.5s; the bar's animation is declared at every width, so crossing 1000px never restarts it). 1.8s after load `app.js` adds `.is-settled`, which drops the entrance animations (their end state is the resting style), so rotating a tablet, resizing across a breakpoint, printing or a full-page capture never replays them from opacity 0. Print turns every animation and transition off (`@media print`), so nothing prints at a first frame; keep that in any print rewrite. Sections reveal once on scroll with `[data-reveal]` only after `app.js` adds `.motion`. The four pillars reveal together with identical timing (never a cascade, which would imply rank).
- Ambient: the aurora layers drift 30s/36s alternate in the cover and the priorities panel; nothing else moves on its own. No ticker, no count-ups, no marquee, no parallax, no scroll-jacking.
- Scroll-driven state (position indicators, so they still update under reduced motion): the header surface and the sliding nav marker (its transition is cut under reduced motion). The marker is measured only when the active section changes, on resize and after the fonts load, never per scroll frame; nothing writes custom properties on `:root` while scrolling.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` cuts every animation and transition, removes the aurora motion, the word reveal, the cursor glow and all hover lifts.
- Glass (`backdrop-filter`) only on the header pill, the glass button and the phone menu sheet. The fact bar, the dark fact tiles and the phone date line are translucent fills without blur (soft light needs none).
- Forced colors: tiles, pillars, cards, the pill, the sheet, the canvas, the phone date line and the Our City band get `CanvasText` borders (the instrument bar gets one border and rules between its cells; the flat voting steps keep their dividers as borders); the band's scrim is removed (the text gets its backplate); the aurora, glow discs, rings, the portrait overlay, the shelf frames' grain and sliding marker are removed (the shelf's draft chips get a border); the tile eyebrow icons take the text colour (the reset names the dark, night and sun tile variants, whose own icon colour is more specific); tight display lines open to 1.08.
- Without JavaScript: everything is visible and static, the header is the dark bordered pill (a plain bar with wrapped links on phones), the form stays behind its noscript note.
